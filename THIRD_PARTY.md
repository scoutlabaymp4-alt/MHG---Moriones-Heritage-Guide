The 3D mask model (shape, paint, hair, three helmet styles, flowers) is original code in `src3d/mask3d.js`. No downloaded 3D model and no museum image is included in the project; the only photograph in it is the logo listed above.
In v8.2 the model's proportions and its three helmet styles were reworked after the group studied 12 photographs of three Moriones masks on the Mask Museum website (maskmuseum.org: "moriones-1", "moriones-2", "moriones-longino"), which the group supplied as visual reference. The photos were looked at only; nothing was traced, scanned, copied or embedded, and the code contains no image data from them. The three styles are simplified, original interpretations of what the photos show in common (red helmet with gold brim band and comb crest; black helmet with gold scrolls and a horsehair mane; woven helmet with flowers). **The licence and ownership of those photographs and of the masks' designs have not been verified, and the carvers of the masks are not credited because their names are not known to us.** Before the public launch, the group should ask the Mask Museum for permission to cite them as reference, and ask a local mask carver to review the result.

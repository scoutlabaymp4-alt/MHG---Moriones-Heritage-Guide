The 3D mask model itself (shape, paint, hair, helmet, flowers) is original code in `src3d/mask3d.js`; no downloaded model, photograph or museum image is included.
The Mask Museum page for a Moriones mask (maskmuseum.org) was suggested as a reference. Its terms could not be verified from this environment, so it was **not** used. To base a model on it, obtain written permission or licence terms first and record them here and in `AI_DISCLOSURE.md`.
